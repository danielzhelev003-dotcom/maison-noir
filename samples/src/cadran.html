What does UV400 mean?

The lenses block all ultraviolet light up to a wavelength of 400 nanometres, which covers both UVA and UVB. Every Maison Noir lens is UV400, whatever its colour.

Which lens category do I need?

Category 2 lenses suit everyday sun and cloudy days. Category 3 lenses are for bright sun, the beach and the mountains. Both are fine for driving. Category 4 lenses are too dark to drive in, so we do not make them.

How do I measure my head for a hat?

Wrap a soft tape measure around your head, about one finger above your eyebrows and ears. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one.

Can I return something?

Yes. Send back anything unworn within 30 days and we refund you in full. Returns within the EU are free.
